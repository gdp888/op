## What Is a WhatsApp Direct Message?

A WhatsApp direct message (sometimes called a "WhatsApp Direct" or "unsaved number message") is simply a WhatsApp conversation started with a phone number that is not saved in your contacts. The message itself is identical to any other WhatsApp message — it is delivered through the same encrypted protocol, and the recipient sees it the same way.

The only difference is the entry point: instead of selecting a contact from your address book, you use a special link or tool to open the conversation directly.

**Traveling** — Hotels, taxi services, tour operators, and local businesses often share WhatsApp numbers. Keep your contacts clean by messaging directly instead of saving each one.

**Professional networking** — After meeting someone at a conference or event, you might want to send a quick follow-up message on WhatsApp without necessarily adding them to your phone book.

**Temporary situations** — Renting a car, hiring a plumber, ordering catering — any situation where the conversation is temporary and the contact does not need to live in your phone forever.

## Tips for Effective Direct Messages

1. **Always include a country code** — WhatsApp identifies numbers by their international format. For Brazil, start with 55. For the USA and Canada, start with 1. For Russia, start with 7.
2. **Use a pre-filled message** — When contacting someone for the first time, a brief introduction helps. Use OPN Chat's message field to write something like "Hi, I found your listing on OLX and I'm interested."
3. **Be aware of privacy settings** — Some WhatsApp users have settings that restrict who can message them. If they have "My Contacts" selected for who can send messages, your message may not go through unless they add your number.
4. **Double-check the number** — Since you are typing the number manually, make sure it is correct before sending. A wrong digit means your message goes to a stranger.
